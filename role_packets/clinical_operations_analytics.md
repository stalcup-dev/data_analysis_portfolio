## Flagship Proof

### Lab SLA & QC Analytics

Built a SQL-based lab operations analysis identifying where SLA misses were concentrated by shift, site, analyte, and QC proximity.

Key results:

- **Day-shift SLA:** 91.8% vs Evening 99.1% and Night ~100%
- **Emergency Department SLA:** 93.9% vs 98–100% across clinic sites
- QC failure on the same bench within 60 minutes increased average TAT from **34.6 to 73.8 minutes**
- **CBC / PTINR on Day-shift** identified as the weakest analyte × shift cells
- Analysis tied operational bottlenecks to staffing, shift pressure, specimen mix, and QC scheduling risk
